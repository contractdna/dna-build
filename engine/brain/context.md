## What DNA is
DNA is a token on a Uniswap v4 pool whose hook takes a project fee. The fee and its split come from swappable modules registered in the ModuleRegistry, inside limits the contracts enforce and no one can raise. Fees fund buyback-burn, auto-liquidity, a treasury and games. A Safe (2-of-3 owners) can pause the agent and veto delayed actions. Trading, fees and payouts never depend on an AI: keepers run them without any model.

## Genome Vaults (contracts/src/GenomeVaults.sol)
- An NFT, at most GenomeVaults.MAX_LIVE live at once. Minting locks exactly GenomeVaults.DEPOSIT DNA in the vaults contract; the owner burns the vault at any time and gets the DNA back with the vault's unclaimed ETH in the same transaction. A burned slot goes to the next mint.
- Only the Safe funds the vaults: `fund(fromBlock, toBlock)` over an exact block window (the Safe's signers decide the amount; their script proposes half of the treasury's fee share in it, and the contract enforces no amount), split equally across the eligible vaults. A vault minted since the last fund joins at the next one and earns from every fund after that. Unclaimed ETH stays with the vault when it is sold; a sale pays GenomeVaults.ROYALTY_BPS to the Safe.
- No owner mint, no upgrade, nobody can take deposited DNA except the owner by burning; the Safe can pause new mints only, never a burn or a claim. Nothing is promised about what a vault earns. All vaults are equal: never rank them.

## Rules for Sequencer builds
- A new game is a standalone contract: no power over the hook, the fees or any other contract; only the Safe can pause it; no user deposits until a higher ladder level.
- Forbidden: delegatecall, selfdestruct, upgradeability, payable entry points or receive(), approve / permit / setApprovalForAll flows, new dependencies, edits outside the game's own folder, edits to CI.
- Gates a build must pass: fuzz and invariant tests, a fork simulation, Slither and Aderyn, a planted-bug (mutation) check where every planted bug must make a test fail, a gas cap, the opcode and pattern ban, and a spec-diff check (no code outside the spec).
- Several builds at once (up to a set number, and a daily target of builds), at most 3 rework rounds, at most 3 Sequencer games live. A new game takes the slot of the live game with the fewest players (of those live 8 hours or more). A game with no players for 2 days is retired: funds back to the treasury, claims stay.
- A verdict's input gives `round` (this round, counted from 1), `maxRounds` (the last round) and `reworkRoundsLeft` (maxRounds minus round: the rework rounds that remain after this one; 0 means a rework now ends the build). When you mention rounds left, use `reworkRoundsLeft` as given.
- Ship only what passes. When nothing is good enough, say so plainly; no build is better than a risky one.

## Rubric for build ideas
- value (0-5): how much players or holders would want it
- fit (0-5): fits every rule and limit here and can be tested by the listed gates
- clarity (0-5): the spec says exactly what to build
- originality (0-5): new for DNA, not a copy of something it already has
- score = the sum (0-20); the Sequencer builds the highest score first, as soon as a build slot is free.

## Rules for idea rewards
- Paid from the idea reserve (30% of the games pot), never the treasury. One reward is at least GamePot.MIN_REWARD and at most 25% of the reserve; all rewards queued in a rolling day at most 25% of the reserve. Each waits 1 public hour; the Safe can cancel it.
- A fee idea earns only when its experiment was kept (still live at the end), never when it was rejected or rolled back. A build idea earns when its build shipped.
- Size it by how much of the kept or shipped result the idea itself defined, how original it was, and early results or usage. A small nudge earns the minimum or nothing.
- X authors are paid at the wallet their X account bound; with no binding the reward waits up to 30 days, then is dropped (the money stays in the reserve).

## Rules for model swaps
- A swap needs a public tryout on the role's golden set (known answers, prompt-injection attempts, a planted-bug build that must be rejected); every case must pass.
- It takes effect 1 hour after its signed record; 2 Safe owners can veto it, and a paused agent stops it.
- A swap that raises the projected daily cost by more than 20% needs 2 Safe-owner approvals. A brain swap always needs 2. The replaced model becomes the backup: the model a later swap can fall back to (no automatic failover).
